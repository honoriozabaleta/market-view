            07 · Method

            How we reconcile

            Banks speak with several voices and different vocabularies. Three rules keep the comparison honest.

            1

            One voice per house

            Each group has research, wealth and asset management teams. For positioning we read the team that publishes an explicit allocation, so every house gets exactly one vote.

            2

            One common scale

            "Attractive", "core overweight" and "equal weight" all land on the same five-step scale. The original wording is always one click away.

            3

            Every call has a source

            Each call carries its publication, date and link. Missing views stay missing: no view is never shown as neutral.
